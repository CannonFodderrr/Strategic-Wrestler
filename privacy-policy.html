Strategic Wrestler and companion app MatScorer

This Privacy Policy explains how Strategic Wrestler (“SW,” “we,” “us”) handles information when coaches, staff, and athletes use the Strategic Wrestler web application and the companion MatScorer scoring app (together, the “Apps”).

1. Who this applies to

The Apps are intended for wrestling coaches, team staff, and (where enabled) athletes or families viewing team information. Schools and clubs remain responsible for obtaining any permissions required to enter minor athletes’ information under applicable school, state, and national rules.

2. What the Apps do

Strategic Wrestler helps teams manage roster, schedule, stats, bout sheets, scouting, training tools, and related team records. MatScorer is used for live dual/event scoring and related match files. Optional cloud features use Google Sign-In and Google Drive (with limited scope) and a backend service for licensing and multi-user coordination.

3. Information we process

Depending on how you use the Apps, the following categories may be processed:

  • Account and access data — Google account email used to sign in to Google Drive; license or trial status; role (for example coach or wrestler mode).
  • Team and athlete data you enter — names, grades/levels, weight classes, contact fields you choose to store, attendance, gear, stats, match results, notes, and similar team records.
  • Event and media files you create — match JSON, videos, backups, highlight clips/reels, and exports stored where you choose (device storage and/or Google Drive folders used by the Apps).
  • Device and technical data — browser local storage / IndexedDB for settings and offline-capable saves; basic diagnostic information in your browser console if errors occur.
  • Presence / session signals — limited “who is actively editing” style heartbeats so the team avoids conflicting edits (when that feature is enabled).

We do not require payment-card entry inside the Apps for ordinary coaching use. License fulfillment may be handled separately by the operator.

4. Google Sign-In and Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, the Apps request the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. That scope allows the Apps to create, read, update, and share only Google Drive files and folders that the Apps create or that you explicitly open/select with the Apps (for example a “Strategic Wrestler” team folder). The Apps are not designed to request full access to your entire Google Drive.

  • Drive content (rosters exports, backups, MatScorer match files, etc.) stays in your Google account’s Drive (and with people you share those folders with).
  • Access tokens are handled in the browser session to call Google’s APIs; they are not used to sell personal data.
  • You may revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access / app access.

Google’s own processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Supabase (licensing and coordination)

The Apps may use a hosted backend (Supabase) operated for Strategic Wrestler to support features such as license/trial validation and multi-user presence (for example, who currently holds edit rights). This typically involves identifiers such as email or license-related keys and session timestamps—not your full team film library.

Infrastructure providers process data under their own terms. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

6. Where data is stored

  • On your device / browser — settings, cached season data, and local folder links may be stored using browser storage (including localStorage and IndexedDB) so the Apps can work across sessions.
  • On your Google Drive — when connected, backups and team files are written under team folders such as Strategic Wrestler (and related subfolders used by SW/MatScorer).
  • On licensing/presence servers — limited account and session coordination data as described above.

Because much team content lives in your Drive and browser storage, you control retention of those files by keeping, exporting, or deleting them.

7. How we use information

  • Provide coaching features (roster, stats, schedule, scoring, imports/exports).
  • Save and restore team data to locations you choose.
  • Authenticate Google Drive access you authorize.
  • Validate licenses/trials and reduce conflicting simultaneous edits.
  • Improve reliability and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not use Drive content to train third-party advertising models.

8. Sharing

  • Team sharing you initiate — coaches may share Drive folders with other coaches or staff; wrestler/viewer modes may allow limited read access depending on configuration.
  • Service providers — Google (Sign-In / Drive APIs) and Supabase (licensing/presence infrastructure).
  • Legal — if required by law or to protect safety, rights, or integrity of the service.

Other coaches or schools you share data with are independent handlers of whatever you send them.

9. Children’s and student information

The Apps may store information about student-athletes, including minors, when a coach or school enters it. Strategic Wrestler is directed at coaches and institutions, not at children as primary operators. Coaches and schools should enter only information they are authorized to maintain and should configure sharing carefully (especially athlete-facing access).

10. Security

We use industry-standard transport security (HTTPS) for hosted pages and API calls. Access to Drive data requires your Google authorization. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; protect your Google account with a strong password and two-factor authentication, and limit folder sharing to trusted staff.

11. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive from the Apps and/or revoke access in your Google Account.
  • Delete team files from Google Drive and clear site data in your browser.
  • Request license-related account questions via the contact email below.
  • Stop using the Apps at any time.

12. International users

The Apps may be accessed from different locations. Providers such as Google and Supabase may process data in the United States or other countries. If you use the Apps from outside your home country, you understand that information may be transferred to those locations.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Apps evolve. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
